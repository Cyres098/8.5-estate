Our Story

Founded on discretion and detail.

8.5 Estate Properties began with a simple conviction: that buying or renting a home in Ghana should feel as considered as the property itself. What started as a boutique advisory in Accra has grown into one of the country's most trusted names in luxury real estate — without ever losing the personal, private service that defined us.

Today we represent landmark residences, penthouses and commercial assets across Greater Accra and the Eastern Region, guiding local families, diaspora buyers and institutional investors through every step.

Our Mission

To connect discerning clients with exceptional properties through honest counsel, rigorous due diligence and uncompromising service.

Our Vision

To be West Africa's most respected luxury real estate house — the first name that comes to mind for an address that matters.
